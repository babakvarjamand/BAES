# BAES

## Babak AI Engineering Standard

**A vendor-neutral engineering standard for systematically designing, governing, and evaluating Human–AI collaboration for real-world problem solving across domains.**

---

> **The BAES Repository is the Public Engineering Record of the project.**

---

## What is BAES?

BAES is an engineering standard for structuring Human–AI collaboration.

It is designed to address the engineering relationships between:

* Human intent
* Authority
* Delegation
* Scope
* AI participation
* Actions
* Evidence
* Evaluation
* Governance
* Outcomes
* Reviewability

BAES is **domain-general** and is not limited to software engineering.

[Read: What is BAES?](docs/what-is-baes.md)

---

## Why Does BAES Exist?

AI systems are increasingly participating in complex real-world problem solving.

However, AI capability alone does not establish:

* Authority
* Scope
* Governance
* Evidence
* Accountability
* Traceability
* Reviewability

BAES is being developed to provide an engineering structure for these concerns.

[Read: Why does BAES exist?](docs/why-does-baes-exist.md)

## Current Status

BAES is currently **under active development**.

The project has established a foundational body of concepts and principles and is currently progressing through foundation consolidation, ontology discovery, conceptual formalization, and development of the supporting evidence and governance models.

BAES has **not yet been released as a finalized normative standard**.

[Read: Current Status](docs/current-status.md)

## Core Development Direction

BAES development currently emphasizes:

* Human Engineering Authority
* Controlled Delegation
* No Autonomous Scope Expansion
* Documentation First
* Traceability
* Evidence-Aware Development
* Independent Reviewability
* Controlled Evolution

These concepts remain subject to continued formalization, research, and validation.

---

## Domain Generality

BAES is not restricted to a particular profession, technology, or application.

Potential application domains include:

* Software engineering
* Scientific research
* Education
* Technical problem solving
* Knowledge work
* Analysis and decision support
* Engineering disciplines
* Other structured Human–AI collaboration contexts

Domain-specific requirements may be layered onto the common BAES foundation where necessary.

---

## Development Philosophy

BAES is being developed according to the engineering principles it seeks to establish.

The project therefore emphasizes:

> **Documentation before implementation.**

> **Evidence before normative claims.**

> **Explicit authority over implicit authority.**

> **Traceability over assumption.**

> **Controlled scope evolution over uncontrolled expansion.**

> **Reviewability over opacity.**

## Project Maturity

BAES is currently a **developmental engineering standard project**.

The project distinguishes between:

```text
Concept / Decision Status
           ≠
Evidence Validity
```

A candidate concept is not automatically a validated principle, and a principle is not automatically a normative requirement.

The standard is intended to evolve through documented inquiry, evidence, validation, review, and controlled formalization.
