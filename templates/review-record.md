## 31. Record Integrity

This record documents a review and its findings.

A review does not automatically establish:

- Truth of every reviewed claim
- Evidence validity
- A BAES principle
- A normative requirement
- Scope authority
- Publication authority
- A formal BAES release

The review outcome must be interpreted within its stated scope,
criteria, evidence, and limitations.

---

## 32. Final Principle

> **A review should make both the strengths and weaknesses of an
> artifact visible before that artifact is relied upon for further
> engineering decisions.**
