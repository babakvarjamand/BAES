## 31. Record Integrity

This research record documents a research activity and its results.

It does not automatically establish:

- Evidence validity
- A BAES principle
- A normative requirement
- A change to BAES scope
- A formal BAES release

Each of these requires its own appropriate evaluation and governance
process.
