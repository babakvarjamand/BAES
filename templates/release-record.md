## 33. Record Integrity

This record documents the creation and publication status of a BAES
release.

A release record does not automatically establish:

- Approval
- Evidence validity
- A new principle
- A normative requirement
- Scope authority
- Governance authority
- Conformance
- Correctness of every released artifact

Those properties require their respective processes and evidence.

---

## 34. Final Principle

> **A release should make it possible to reconstruct what was published,
> why it was published, what authorized it, what was reviewed, and what
> limitations were known at the time of publication.**
