## 39. Record Integrity

This case study records an evaluation context and its findings.

It does not automatically establish:

- Evidence validity
- A BAES principle
- A normative requirement
- A conformance rule
- A change to BAES scope
- A formal BAES release

Such outcomes require their respective evaluation and governance
processes.

## 40. Final Principle
