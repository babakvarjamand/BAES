## 29. Record Integrity

This record documents a proposed or completed change to BAES or one of
its public engineering artifacts.

A Change Record does not automatically authorize execution.

In particular, the existence of this record does not itself establish:

- Approval
- Authority
- Evidence validity
- Scope expansion
- A new principle
- A normative requirement
- A formal release

Those outcomes require the appropriate project process.

---

## 30. Final Principle

> **Every significant change should be reconstructable:
> what changed, why it changed, who authorized it, what supported it,
> and what resulted from it.**
